📺 Cast to your TV

  1. Put your laptop and Chromecast on the same Wi‑Fi network.
  2. In Chrome, open the menu (⋮) and choose Cast, save and share → Cast… (the wording varies a little between Chrome versions).
  3. In the Cast window, open Sources and choose Cast tab, then click your Chromecast.
  4. Come back here, press Test sound to check the TV is playing audio, then Start workout and Fullscreen (or press F).
Websites can't open Chrome's Cast window themselves, which is why the steps above happen in Chrome's menu. Tab casting also sends this tab's sound to the TV. Keep this tab open while you train, and control the timer from the laptop.
